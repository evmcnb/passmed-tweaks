# Passmed Tweaks: Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 21 November 2026_

Passmed Tweaks is an unofficial browser extension for passmedicine.com. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Passmedicine.

## What it stores
- **Your settings** (theme, toggles) in your browser's extension storage. If browser sync is on, your browser may sync these between your own devices.
- **An answer log** (question ID, time, whether you were right, time taken, category, the public "% of users correct" figure, topic title) and, for questions you got wrong, the question text, options, answer and explanation, so the stats page and Anki export work. This is kept in local extension storage on your device only.

## What it sends
Nothing. The extension has no servers, analytics, trackers or third-party services. Its only network requests go to passmedicine.com, using the same endpoint the site itself uses to load question data, so it can show statistics on the review list.

## Your control
Turn off "Log my answers" in the popup to stop logging. "Clear data" on the My stats page deletes the log. Uninstalling the extension removes everything it stored.
